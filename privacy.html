Seedtone Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-12

Seedtone does not collect any personal data. What you create in the app is stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. We cannot access it.

We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking. Purchases are handled by Apple.

To delete your data, delete it in the app.

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page.

Contact: way_u_support@icloud.com
